# Project Instructions

## Design Contract

- Before any UI, CSS, SCSS, template or visual asset work, read `CONTRAT_DESIGN.md`.
- The approved public-homepage visual reference is
  `docs/design/accueil-reference-validee-2026-10-16.png`. Its vehicle-brand strip has
  a light-gray background, not a blue one. Follow the contract's scope and exact palette.
- Follow its approved palette: white `#FFFFFF`, light gray `#F5F6F8`, anthracite
  `#232B38`, brand blue `#315FB7`, action orange `#FF8A00`.
- Keep the user's approved slide images, information, functional navigation and
  sticky public header. Do not substitute generated artwork without explicit approval.
- Preserve business logic and access rules during visual work.
- Apply changes only within the requested scope. Do not treat the contract as an
  instruction to recolor unrelated legacy screens or revert existing user changes.
- Validate affected behavior and desktop/mobile rendering before delivery; report
  any testing or deployment limitations accurately.
